Quality & Technology

The four things a ball has to get right.

Precision, consistency, durability and control. Everything we specify serves one of the four — and we would rather explain the trade-offs than pretend there are none.

PrecisionPanel accuracy and sphericity
ConsistencyBall to ball, batch to batch
DurabilitySeam and surface life
ControlTouch, grip and flight

Exploded view

Four layers, outside in.

Drag the ball to turn it. Each layer below is a separate decision, and each one changes how the finished ball plays.

01 · Outer Layer

PU or TPU. Sets touch, grip and how the print sits. Grain and thickness are specified per range.

02 · Panels

32-panel, low-panel or 20-panel layouts. Panel geometry drives flight character and artwork area.

03 · Lining

Layers of backing cloth between outer and bladder. This is what holds the ball round under repeated impact.

04 · Bladder

Butyl for air retention, latex for rebound. Chosen against how often the ball gets inflated.

Inspection

What we check before it ships.

These are the checks we run in-house. We do not claim third-party certification unless a specific order has been certified — ask us and we will tell you exactly what applies.

Sphericity

Roundness measured across multiple axes on inflated balls.

Circumference

Checked against the nominal size for the specified size class.

Weight

Verified to the size standard agreed at order.

Air Retention

Pressure held over time, to confirm bladder and valve seating.

Seam Integrity

Stitch or bond strength inspected around the full ball.

Print & Colour

Registration and colour matched against your approved proof.
